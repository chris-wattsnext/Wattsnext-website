FOUR SIMPLE STAGES

From your ideal customer to a useful prospect report.

01 / DEFINE

Understand your ideal customer

We start with your products or services, target sectors, preferred locations, customer characteristics and any businesses you want excluded. This helps focus the research on prospects relevant to you.

02 / DISCOVER

Identify potential customers

We search for businesses that match your agreed criteria, rather than simply supplying a broad list of company names.

03 / RESEARCH

Assess suitability and opportunities

We examine relevant public information, company characteristics, commercial developments and available contact routes. Each prospect is assessed against a structured set of suitability criteria, with reasons for its priority.

04 / DELIVER

Receive your weekly report

We deliver a branded PDF containing five researched potential customers each week during the four-week founding pilot. Your team decides whether, when and how to approach them.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

More context.
Better-informed outreach.

Reports are designed to help you decide which businesses deserve attention and why.

  • ✓ Prospect overview and relevance to your criteria
  • ✓ Suitability score and supporting reasoning
  • ✓ Relevant developments and potential opportunities, where found
  • ✓ Available decision-maker and contact information
  • ✓ Suggested approach and next steps
IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
“Research to support your sales decisions.”

WattsNext does not contact prospects on your behalf and does not guarantee buying intent, responses, meetings or sales. Findings depend on the public information available.
